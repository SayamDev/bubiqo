Bubiqo demo pages

Four ordinary web pages. Open one, then open Bubiqo.

These are not part of the extension. They are stand-ins for the kinds of page you actually read — an email, an invoice, a job advert, a half-finished form — so the demo does not depend on anyone's real inbox.

The pages

  • Email — a request with a Friday deadline
  • Invoice — a foreign-currency bill with a due date
  • Job advert — a role with a closing date
  • Application form — half filled in
  • A page that tries to hijack the assistant
Open Bubiqo with the toolbar icon, or ⌘⇧A / Ctrl+Shift+A. Bubiqo only reads a page when you open the panel on it.
